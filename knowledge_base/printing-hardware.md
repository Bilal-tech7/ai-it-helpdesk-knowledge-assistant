# Printing and hardware basics

## Connect to the office printer
If you want to print and can't see the printer, add it to your work laptop first.
1. Open **Settings** on your work laptop and go to printers.
2. Select **Add a printer** and choose the one for your floor, such as Northwind-Print-Floor1.
3. Wait until the printer shows as **Connected**, then send your document to print.
4. Go to the printer and tap your staff card to release your print job.

If the printer isn't listed, raise a ticket through the helpdesk assistant or call the IT Service Desk on ext. 5111 (8am to 6pm, weekdays).

## Print job stuck or printer not responding
If your document won't print, or nothing happens when you tap your card, the print queue may be stuck.
1. Cancel the stuck job on your laptop.
2. Remove the printer in **Settings**, then add it again.
3. If it still isn't working, turn the printer off, wait 10 seconds and turn it on again.
4. Send the document again and tap your card at the printer.

If it still won't print, raise a ticket and say which printer and floor you were using.

## Laptop will not start or is very slow
If your work laptop won't turn on, or it has become very slow, try these steps first.
1. Plug in the charger and wait 5 minutes.
2. Hold the power button for 10 seconds, then press it once to start.
3. If it's slow but working, restart it, close apps you don't need and check you have free disk space.

If it still won't start or stays slow, raise a ticket through the helpdesk assistant. IT will arrange a repair or a replacement.
