# Password reset, lockouts and MFA

## Reset a forgotten password
If you can't remember your Northwind password or can't sign in to your account, you can reset it yourself.
1. Go to the Northwind Portal sign-in page.
2. Select **Forgot password**.
3. Enter your work email address.
4. Approve the sign-in request in the Northwind Authenticator app on your phone.
5. Choose a new password with at least 12 characters. Passwords must be changed every 90 days.

If you don't receive the approval request or the reset fails, raise a ticket through the helpdesk assistant or call the IT Service Desk on ext. 5111 (8am to 6pm, weekdays).

## Change your password before it expires
Northwind passwords must be changed every 90 days. If you remember your current password and want to change it, you don't need to reset it.
1. Sign in to the Northwind Portal.
2. Open **Account settings** and select **Change password**.
3. Enter your current password, then choose a new one with at least 12 characters.

If you can't sign in or the change fails, raise a ticket through the helpdesk assistant or call the IT Service Desk on ext. 5111 (8am to 6pm, weekdays).

## Account locked after too many attempts
If you're locked out of your Northwind account because you entered the wrong password too many times, the lock is temporary.
1. Stop trying to sign in. Your account locks after 5 wrong password attempts.
2. Wait 30 minutes. The account unlocks automatically.
3. Sign in again with your correct password. If you've forgotten it, use **Forgot password** on the Northwind Portal.

If you're still locked out after 30 minutes, raise a ticket through the helpdesk assistant or call the IT Service Desk on ext. 5111 (8am to 6pm, weekdays).

## Lost or replaced phone (MFA)
If you've lost your phone or got a new one and can't approve sign-ins to your Northwind account, you'll need IT to reset your MFA. You can't sign in without MFA, and there is no backup sign-in method.
1. Contact the IT Service Desk straight away by raising a ticket through the helpdesk assistant or calling ext. 5111 (8am to 6pm, weekdays). If your phone is lost, say so, so IT can secure your account.
2. IT will confirm your identity and reset your MFA.
3. Install the Northwind Authenticator app on your new phone (App Store for iPhone, Google Play for Android).
4. Sign in to the Northwind Portal and follow the prompts to link your account to the app.

If linking fails or you get an error, raise another ticket.

## What IT will never ask
IT staff at Northwind will never ask you for your password or your MFA code, whether by phone, email or message. This applies to anyone who claims to be from IT.
1. Don't share your password or MFA code with anyone.
2. If someone asks for them on a call, hang up. If it's a message or email, don't reply or click any links.
3. Report it straight away to the IT Service Desk by raising a ticket or calling ext. 5111.
