# Wi-Fi and network connectivity

## Connect to office Wi-Fi
If you can't connect to the office Wi-Fi or your laptop or phone can't get online, connect to the Northwind-Staff network.
1. Turn Wi-Fi on in your device settings.
2. Select the **Northwind-Staff** network from the list.
3. Sign in with your work email and your Northwind password.
4. If it fails, select **Forget network**, then reconnect from step 2.
5. If it still fails, restart your device and try again.

Do not use Northwind-Guest. It is for visitors and cannot reach internal systems. If you still can't connect, raise a ticket through the helpdesk assistant or call the IT Service Desk on ext. 5111 (8am to 6pm, weekdays).

## Wi-Fi connected but no internet or keeps dropping
If your wifi shows as connected but pages won't load, or your connection is slow or keeps dropping, there is usually a quick fix.
1. Turn Wi-Fi off, wait a few seconds, and turn it on again.
2. Move closer to a Wi-Fi access point, since a weak signal causes slow speeds and dropouts.
3. Select **Forget network** for Northwind-Staff and reconnect with your work email and password.
4. Restart your device.

If the problem continues, or it affects other people nearby, raise a ticket through the helpdesk assistant or call the IT Service Desk on ext. 5111 (8am to 6pm, weekdays). Tell them where in the office you were and when it happened.

## Connect a visitor to guest Wi-Fi
If a visitor needs internet access in the office, they can use the Northwind-Guest network. It does not give access to internal systems.
1. Ask reception for today's guest Wi-Fi code. The code changes every day.
2. On the visitor's device, select the **Northwind-Guest** network.
3. Enter the daily code when asked.

Employees should use Northwind-Staff, not the guest network. If the guest code doesn't work, ask reception for the code again, or raise a ticket through the helpdesk assistant or call the IT Service Desk on ext. 5111 (8am to 6pm, weekdays).
