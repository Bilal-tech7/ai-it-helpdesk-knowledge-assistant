# VPN and remote access

## Connect to the VPN from home
If you're working from home and need to reach internal Northwind systems, connect to the Northwind VPN first.
1. Make sure your internet connection is working.
2. Open the **Northwind VPN** app on your work laptop.
3. Sign in with your work email and password.
4. Approve the sign-in request in the Northwind Authenticator app on your phone.
5. Wait for the app to show **Connected**, then open the system you need.

If you can't sign in or don't receive the approval request, raise a ticket through the helpdesk assistant or call the IT Service Desk on ext. 5111 (8am to 6pm, weekdays).

## VPN not connecting or keeps disconnecting
If the Northwind VPN won't connect, or it connects and then drops, there is usually a quick fix.
1. Check you can open a normal website. If you can't, fix your home internet first.
2. Quit the Northwind VPN app completely and open it again.
3. Restart your laptop and try again.
4. If it keeps disconnecting, move closer to your home router or use a cable if you can.

If the problem continues, raise a ticket through the helpdesk assistant or call the IT Service Desk on ext. 5111 (8am to 6pm, weekdays). Tell them what error you see and when it started.

## Do I need the VPN in the office
You only need the Northwind VPN when you're working remotely. When you're connected to the office Northwind-Staff Wi-Fi, you can reach internal systems without it.
1. In the office, connect to Northwind-Staff and use your systems as normal.
2. At home or travelling, connect to the VPN first.

If you're in the office and still can't reach an internal system, raise a ticket through the helpdesk assistant or call the IT Service Desk on ext. 5111 (8am to 6pm, weekdays).
